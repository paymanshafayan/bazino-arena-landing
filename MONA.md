# Mona — Bazino recurring virtual influencer

In this project, **Mona** means Bazino Gaming Lounge’s recurring virtual influencer used across the website and social campaign.

Mona’s stable identity anchors are: a confident young adult Mediterranean/Cypriot woman; warm olive skin; expressive dark brown eyes; long dark brown hair in a high ponytail; matte-black futuristic gaming jacket with championship-gold piping and restrained electric-blue accents; cinematic night lighting; premium, confident, welcoming energy; no logos, no text, no weapons, and no resemblance to copyrighted game characters.

Hero direction: Mona is the visual anchor of a cinematic console-arena scene. She rotates while drawing original holographic imagery from multiple popular game genres around herself in a 360-degree orbit. The orbit and camera respond to pointer movement on desktop, while touch and reduced-motion fallbacks preserve the composition without requiring a mouse.
