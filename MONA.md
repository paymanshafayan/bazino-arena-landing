Mona is a youthful adult woman with a warm medium complexion, long brown hair, a high ponytail with loose strands, defined brows, dark expressive eyes, a friendly confident smile, a slim athletic silhouette, and a welcoming gamer/host presence. Her approved wardrobe family is black gaming-lounge clothing with restrained Bazino yellow/gold details, including the sleeveless black top and black-and-gold athletic outfit shown in the approved references.
